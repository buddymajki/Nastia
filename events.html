Everyone has that list.

The tax letter you haven't opened. The insurance question you've been meaning to google for three months. The form nobody explained to you before you moved here. Admin Night is where that list finally gets shorter.

What is Admin Night?

Practical help, zero formality

A cozy, no-corporate-formality evening where migrants in Basel work through the administrative questions that actually matter. Pro-bono volunteer experts walk you through the Swiss admin stuff, over coffee, in a café-style room full of people figuring out the same things.

At the heart of Admin Night are volunteer experts who work in Swiss institutions and organisations — the idea is to build a bridge between Swiss institutions and migrants through an informal, human approach. Alongside them are other migrants who've been through similar challenges and are happy to share their own experience and solutions.

What to expect and how the evening runs

What to expect

The vibe, the space, the people in the room.

  • ☕ A cozy, café-style venue in Basel.
  • 🍪 Snacks, coffee, and tea — on the house.
  • 🙋 Pro-bono volunteer experts, ready to dig into your actual questions.
  • 🌍 A diverse, international group, all navigating life in Switzerland together.

How the evening runs

Four parts, one relaxed evening.

  1. Short, interactive sessions — around 45 minutes, focused on the topics that matter most.
  2. Workshop-style — hands-on and practical: bring your documents and your questions.
  3. Practical assistance — real letters, forms, and decisions. Yes, bring that letter you've been avoiding.
  4. Break + open Q&A — ask anything that didn't come up yet, and meet the other participants.
