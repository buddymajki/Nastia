Support us

Keep Admin Night free.

BOOST Integration runs on volunteers, borrowed rooms and small budgets. Your support pays for real things: room hire for each evening, snacks and coffee, printed materials. It keeps Admin Night free for the people who need it.

Volunteer

Give an evening. Change someone's year.

Admin Night runs on two kinds of boosts — and both are volunteers:

❤️ Practical, pro-bono boost — you work in a Swiss institution or organisation and can help solve concrete problems: taxes, insurance, housing, law, banking.

🤝 Peer boost — you've been through the Swiss admin maze yourself and can explain how it works, in plain words.

Partner with us

Foundations, institutions, employers

We're building a bridge between Swiss institutions and migrant communities — and we're actively looking for partners who want to build it with us.

  • Fund a season of Admin Night — venue, materials, coffee included.
  • Offer expertise — let your specialists host a session pro bono.
  • Share space — a café-style room in Basel for one evening a month.
  • Reach your people — help us reach labour migrants through your channels.

Our research shows exactly where the gap is. Let's close it together.
