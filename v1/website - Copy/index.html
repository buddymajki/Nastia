The part nobody warns you about

Settled on paper. Still lost in the system.

Most integration support is built for the first months after arrival. But the hard questions don't stop after year one — they just get quieter.

The unopened letter

The tax letter you haven't opened. The insurance question you've been meaning to google for three months. The form nobody explained to you before you moved here. Everyone has that list.

The quiet loneliness

Around three in four people in their second year here still feel lonely at least sometimes. Building real friendships in a new country takes more than time — it takes places to meet.

The workaround

When something's unclear, most people don't call an office — they ask a friend, a group chat, the internet. Support should live where people actually look for it.

We listened first

103 conversations across Basel

In July 2026, our volunteer team surveyed migrants and foreign residents across the Basel region — on Barfüsserplatz, along the Rhine, at Roche, at the University of Basel. Here's what they told us.

Read the full research →

Street interviews in Basel, July 2026.

79% hadn't contacted any integration organisation in the past 12 months
~75% of people in their second year felt lonely at least sometimes
56% rely on personal networks — friends, social media, the internet — not official channels
3.4/5 average rating people gave their experiences with integration organisations
What BOOST does

A civic onboarding platform. In human words: a boost.

BOOST Integration connects migrants of all backgrounds with essential integration resources, peer-to-peer support from people who've navigated the same journey, and pro-bono volunteer experts — to foster meaningful integration into Swiss society.

Knowledge

Essential resources

Practical guidance for the Swiss admin maze — taxes, insurance, housing, banking, rules — explained by people who actually deal with it.

People

Peer-to-peer support

"I've been through this myself and can explain how it works." Migrants helping migrants — because the best guide is someone a few steps ahead.

Experts

Pro-bono professionals

"I can directly help you solve a concrete problem." Volunteer experts from Swiss institutions and organisations, ready to dig into your actual questions.

One relaxed evening. That list — finally sorted.

Admin Night is a cozy, no-corporate-formality evening where migrants in Basel work through the administrative questions that actually matter — over coffee, with pro-bono experts and people figuring out the same things.

  • ☕ A cozy, café-style venue in Basel — snacks, coffee and tea on the house
  • 🙋 Pro-bono volunteer experts, ready to dig into your actual questions
  • 🌍 A diverse, international group, all navigating life in Switzerland together
  • 📄 Hands-on: bring your documents — yes, that letter you've been avoiding
