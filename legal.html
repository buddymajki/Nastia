Privacy

This website is intentionally minimal about data:

  • No cookies. This site sets no cookies.
  • No tracking or analytics. We don't use analytics tools or embed third-party trackers.
  • No forms that store data. The contact form only opens a draft in your own email app — nothing is transmitted to or stored on this website.
  • Self-hosted assets. Fonts and images are served from this website, not from third-party services.

If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply and handle your request.
