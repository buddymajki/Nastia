About us

Built by migrants, for migrants.

Boost Integration is a civic onboarding platform that connects migrants of all backgrounds with essential integration resources, peer-to-peer support from those who have navigated the same journey, and pro-bono volunteers — to foster meaningful integration into Swiss society.

Our mission

Closing the opportunity gap

To reduce the opportunity gap for migrants by providing access to the knowledge, community, networks, and guidance they need to integrate into Swiss society.

We're an association (Verein) based in Basel. Our starting point wasn't a strategy paper — it was a survey. In July 2026 we went out and asked 103 migrants and foreign residents about their real needs and challenges. What we heard became our research, and our first programme: Admin Night.

Basel's Rhine steps — where a lot of our conversations started.

What we boost

More than paperwork

Integration support usually means forms and offices. We think a settled life is built from more than that:

Confidence Courage Potential Independence Diversity Social Capital Awareness Connection
